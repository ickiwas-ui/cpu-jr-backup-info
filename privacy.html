CPU Jr. Backup Privacy Policy

CPU Jr. Backup is a personal application for backing up a Raspberry Pi to Google Drive.

Google Drive access is used to create, manage, retrieve, and delete application backup files.

Backup contents are encrypted locally before upload to Google Drive.

Google OAuth authorization credentials are stored locally on the owner's Raspberry Pi.

Data is not sold or used for advertising. Encrypted backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account until deleted.

The owner may revoke Google Drive access through Google Account security settings and delete backup files directly.
